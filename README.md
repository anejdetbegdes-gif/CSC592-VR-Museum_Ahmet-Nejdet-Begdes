# CSC 592 – Assignment 2: VR Museum

## History of Cybersecurity VR Museum

This project is an immersive VR museum focused on the history and evolution of cybersecurity.

The museum allows users to explore a virtual exhibition space using VR controllers, interact with selected exhibit objects, and view information panels when approaching exhibitions.

The topic was selected to show how cybersecurity has evolved from early cryptography and computer worms to modern concepts such as Zero Trust and AI Security.

## Exhibitions

The museum includes 10 exhibitions:

1. Enigma Machine
2. Creeper Virus
3. Morris Worm
4. First Firewall
5. RSA Encryption
6. ILOVEYOU Worm
7. Stuxnet
8. WannaCry
9. Zero Trust
10. Modern AI Security

## VR Features

- Meta XR Camera Rig
- VR controller tracking
- Smooth locomotion
- Snap turning
- Controller-based grab interaction on selected exhibits
- Proximity-based information panels
- Closed museum environment with lighting and exhibition stands

## Bonus Features

### Bonus 1: 10+ Exhibitions

The museum includes 10 cybersecurity exhibitions.

### Bonus 2: Exhibition Animations

At least two exhibitions include animations to make the museum more interactive.
