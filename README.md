## History of Cybersecurity VR Museum

This project is an immersive VR museum focused on the history and evolution of cybersecurity.

The museum allows users to explore a virtual exhibition space using VR controllers, interact with selected exhibit objects, and view information panels when approaching exhibitions.

The topic was selected to show how cybersecurity has evolved from early cryptography and computer worms to modern concepts such as Zero Trust and AI Security.

## Exhibitions

The museum includes 10 exhibitions:

1. **Enigma Machine** – World War II encryption machine and an important milestone in cryptanalysis.
2. **Creeper Virus** – One of the earliest computer worms, created in 1971.
3. **Morris Worm** – A major 1988 internet worm that infected thousands of systems.
4. **First Firewall** – Represents the early development of network traffic filtering and perimeter security.
5. **RSA Encryption** – Public-key encryption method introduced in 1977 for secure communication and digital signatures.
6. **ILOVEYOU Worm** – Email-based worm that spread worldwide in 2000.
7. **Stuxnet** – Malware that demonstrated how cyberattacks could affect industrial control systems.
8. **WannaCry** – Global ransomware attack that encrypted files and demanded payment in 2017.
9. **Zero Trust** – Modern security model based on the principle of "never trust, always verify."
10. **Modern AI Security** – Shows how artificial intelligence is used for both cyber defense and emerging attacks.

## VR Features

- Meta XR Camera Rig
- VR controller tracking
- Smooth locomotion using VR controllers
- Snap turning
- Controller-based grab interaction on selected exhibits
- Proximity-based information panels
- Closed museum environment with lighting and exhibition stands

### Bonus 2: Exhibition Animations

Two exhibitions include animations:

- **Enigma Machine** – includes a continuous rotation animation.
- **Stuxnet** – includes a floating up-and-down animation.

These animations make the museum environment more interactive and visually dynamic.
